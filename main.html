Old Town

Where old shophouses, street murals, and Concubine Lane's laneways all sit within a short walk of each other.

Sights & wandering
Laneways

Second & Third Concubine Lane

Two lanes filled with small shops selling souvenirs and murals depicting the local heritage. Good for a leisurely stroll.

Google Maps
Street art

Ipoh Mural

Painted artworks tucked into the old town's back alleys — easy to come across while exploring Concubine Lane.

Nature & Temples

Limestone hills, cave temples, and a couple of easy spots for a stroll.

A slower pace
Nature

Gunung Lang Recreational Park

A nature park good for a family stroll. Pay to take a boat across the lake and feed fish. Skippable if time is tight.

Time needed: 30 min – 1 hr
Google Maps
Cave temple

Perak Cave Temple

A Buddhist temple built into the hillside, founded in 1926 by a monk from China. A short drive from Gunung Lang.

Google Maps
Hike

Kledang Hill

A good spot for a mountain hike; go very early to catch it at its best.

Best time: Very early morning, before the sun rises
Google Maps
Cave temple

Sam Poh Tong Temple

The oldest cave temple in Ipoh, established in 1912. Pairs well with nearby Tasik Cermin.

Google Maps
Park

Tasik Cermin

A photogenic lake for a short explore. Pairs well with Tasik Cermin 2 further up the road.

Best time: Early morning, avoids the heat and the crowds
Google Maps
Adventure

Tasik Cermin 2

Rock climbing, or a boat tour through the cave. Pair with a coffee stop at Kin Loong Valley Chang Jiang beforehand.

Best time: Early morning, avoids the heat and the crowds
Google Maps
Cave temple

Kek Lok Tong Temple

A large cave temple with a Chinese garden, koi ponds, and a walk-through tunnel to a bamboo grove out back.

Google Maps

Full-day trip

Cameron Highlands

Good for an entire day trip, though not ideal if you're short on time — it's also the rainy season in November. The road up is winding, so drive slow.

Google Maps
Historic

Kellie's Castle

A century-old castle built by a Scottish owner — about 30–45 min drive. Worth visiting for its architecture.

Where to Eat

Ipoh's famous for its white coffee, dim sum, and curry mee: here's where to start.

Grouped by meal

Breakfast & Kopi

Go early: dim sum sells out

Ming Court Hong Kong Dimsum

Old-school dim sum where servers come out with trays. Great taste — go early to beat the queues.

Getting there
By Air
  • SG → Ipoh: 1 hr direct flight
  • SG → KL: 1 hr flight, then KL → Ipoh by train (~2 hrs)
  • JB → Ipoh: 1 hr direct flight (cheaper than flying from SG)
By Land
  • Train from JB → Ipoh (~7 hours)
  • By car (~7–8 hours)
